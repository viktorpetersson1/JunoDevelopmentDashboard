# Atlas Tooltip Copy (T103.6)

Plain-English explanations for every key metric. Peter or Lars should be able
to hover and read two sentences that explain the number — no jargon, no formulae.

## Dashboard chips

### Next capital call

"The earliest upcoming draw on the KPC family-office line of credit or
a Harrison project-finance facility across all active projects. Date and
amount are derived from the calc engine's monthly debt-draw schedule."

### Next owner distribution

"Your projected share of the next project closing — calculated as your
cap-table percentage times the forecast Net Profit After Tax for the
project closest to completion. Actual payment depends on final sale price."

### KPC LOC headroom

"How much of the $X KPC family-office line of credit is still available.
Drawn = total debt currently outstanding across all projects. Red when
utilisation exceeds 90%."

### Rollout pacing

"When we need to START the next project to keep trailing-12-month NPAT at
or above the exec target. Based on the typical 18-month project-to-NPAT
cycle and the current pipeline's closing schedule."

## Project Summary tab

### NPAT margin

"Net Profit After Tax divided by Gross Revenue. This is the percentage of
sale proceeds that end up as after-tax profit — the cleanest single
measure of a project's efficiency."

### IRR (Internal Rate of Return)

"The annualised return on the equity invested in this project. Computed
by the calc engine's IRR bisection from the monthly equity-drawn and
equity-returned series."

### MOIC

"Multiple on Invested Capital — total equity returned divided by total
equity drawn. A MOIC of 1.87× means for every $1 of equity put in, $1.87
comes back (before tax)."

### Rollout Pacing (on Summary)

"This is when we need to start the next project so trailing-12-month NPAT
stays at or above the exec target ($X). Based on the current pipeline
closing schedule and a typical 18-month project-to-NPAT cycle. Click to
advance a candidate in /pipeline."

## Pricing tab

### Staleness banner

"The newest closed comp in the library is more than 6 months old. The AI
pricing recommendation is still valid but may underweight recent market
movement. Click Refresh comps to pull fresh data."

## /analytics/sensitivity

### Tornado chart

"Each bar shows how much the portfolio's pre-tax profit changes when one
input is moved from its 10th to 90th percentile — everything else held
constant. Longer bar = more sensitive to that driver."
